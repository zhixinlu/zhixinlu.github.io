Research

A dynamical-systems and stochastic-process foundation for intelligence — spanning theory, algorithms, and neuroscience.

Dynamical AI

How do learning machines infer, store, and reproduce dynamics? I study recurrent neural networks and reservoir computers as dynamical systems, showing how they learn global temporal structure from local examples, reconstruct chaotic attractors, and implement implicit learning through invertible generalized synchronization. More recently, I develop energy-landscape and geometric views of modern architectures such as transformers.
