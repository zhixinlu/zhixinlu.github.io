Attention as Energy-Landscape Hopping

Presented at the Santa Fe Institute
Invited talk & visiting scientist — May 2026 · Host: Yuanzhao Zhang

This interactive essay re-derives single-head self-attention as one step of the Concave–Convex Procedure (CCCP) on a Gaussian-mixture energy landscape, and shows that the update is geometrically a “hop” along the surface normal followed by a fixed linear readout. It also covers the tanh and linear kernel variants, with a live demo.
