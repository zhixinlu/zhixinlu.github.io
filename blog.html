Attention as Energy-Landscape Hopping

Santa Fe Institute · Part II of the talk · May 2026

A self-contained derivation showing that single-head self-attention is exactly one step of the Concave–Convex Procedure (CCCP) on a Gaussian-mixture energy landscape — geometrically, a "hop" along the surface normal followed by a fixed linear readout. Includes the tanh and linear kernel variants and a live interactive demo.
